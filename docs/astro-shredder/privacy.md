# AstroShredder Privacy Policy

_Last updated: November 10, 2026_

[Español](es/privacy) · [Support](./)

AstroShredder does not collect, store or share any personal data.

## What stays on your device

Your game progress, settings and preferences are saved only on your device. They are never sent to us or to anyone else.

## Bluetooth and MIDI

AstroShredder uses Bluetooth only to find and connect to MIDI keyboards. The notes you play are processed on your device to run the game and are never recorded or transmitted.

## No tracking, ads or analytics

AstroShredder has no accounts, advertising, analytics or third-party tracking, and it does not connect to the internet.

## Children

Because AstroShredder collects no data, it collects no data from children.

## Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new date.
